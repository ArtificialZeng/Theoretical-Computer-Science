# Rights and attribution

The author lists printed in the manuscripts are preserved. Zijian Zeng maintains this repository; the initial five manuscripts retain their coauthors, and the five later releases are sole-authored.

Existing license notices in included files continue to apply. In particular, the official ACM acmart distribution carries its own LaTeX Project Public License notices, and the DRAT-trim/LRAT checker sources carry their MIT license. No new repository-wide license grant is asserted by this synchronization.

Third-party research papers are cited rather than redistributed as full-text caches. The published source archives preserve their original attribution and local release records. AI assistance is disclosed in the manuscripts.

The additional stoc1–stoc5 manuscripts retain their supplied author lists and source archives. The stoc5 ELINA source subset retains its upstream LGPL-3.0 notice; TypeScript dependencies are specified by a lockfile and fetched for reproduction rather than vendored here. The stoc6–stoc8 template inputs retain upstream ACM/LPPL notices. No new repository-wide license grant is inferred from this synchronization.
