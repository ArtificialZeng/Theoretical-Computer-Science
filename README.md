### Truly Subquadratic 3SUM over Every Bounded-Exponent Abelian Group

**Abstract.** Gives a randomized truly subquadratic algorithm for three-list zero-sum detection and witness recovery over every compact finite abelian group of fixed bounded exponent and polynomial order. The exponent saving depends on the exponent bound, independently of the polynomial order parameter. Simultaneous original-coset profiles and an extremal divisor certificate balance all torsion orders around a prior thin-matrix engine. The algorithm has a worst-case time cap and one-sided success probability at least 3/4, and implies subquadratic algorithms for every fixed abelian-group-representable finite coordinate relation.

### Truly Subcubic Exact Triangle over Bounded-Exponent Abelian Groups

**Abstract.** Gives a deterministic truly subcubic Exact Triangle algorithm over every compact finite abelian group of fixed bounded exponent and polynomial order. A capped witness listing constructs enough of a known frequency decomposition without cubic initialization; vertex gauges then cancel quotient carries and reduce the remaining constraints to a smaller-exponent kernel. Explicit construction costs and a recursive exponent ledger make the saving independent of the polynomial group-order parameter. This extends the elementary-group scope without improving integer APSP bounds.

### Rectangular Batching for Disjoint Shortest Paths on DAGs

**Abstract.** Detects k prescribed mutually vertex-disjoint shortest paths in a positive-weight directed acyclic graph, for every fixed k≥3, in deterministic O_k(m+n+n^(k+0.3)) time. ### A Size-Independent Discrepancy Bound for Sparse Coverage Functions

**Abstract.** Can sparse coverage functions be balanced with an error independent of the number of items?
