[C17] | \begin{lemma}[Regular constrained futures]\label{lem:regular} For a normalized pushdown machine and a regular input language $R$, the configurations that can consume a word in $R$ and accept after EOF form an effectively constructible regular set. \end{lemma} \begin{proof} Take the product with an automaton for $R$, updating its state on consuming transitions. Permit EOF only at an accepting $R$ state. A regular target after EOF is represented by an NFA reading stacks top to bottom, with product controls as initial states and separate internal target states. Initially no edge enters a control state. For each pushdown rule $(p,A)\longrightarrow(q,\beta)$, $|\beta|\le2$, add $p\xrightarrow A u$ whenever a current path $q\xrightarrow\beta u$ exists. This saturation adds edges but no states, and terminates in polynomial time~\cite{10.1007/3-540-63141-0_10}. | Attribution | paper-body.tex:89
