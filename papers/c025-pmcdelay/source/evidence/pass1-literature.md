# Bounded primary-literature comparison, 2026-11-01 UTC

Job c025-pmcdelay-research-338d67d18097. This is a researcher comparison, not a fresh referee, priority certificate or STOC assessment.

- Berge–Limouzy–Schivre, [Output-linear time enumeration of potential maximal cliques](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19850), v3 revised 20 October 2026, is still the current version on the live primary record. Actual supplied PDF/text Theorem 1 gives total O(n^4 m |PMC(G)|), and Section 1.4 explicitly leaves polynomial delay open. Lemmas 11/12 supply recursive lifts/seeds; Theorems 8–11/Corollary 3 supply reachability from the full non-a-nice seed family. The new proposed guarantee is all-input polynomial initial/inter-output/final delay. Its proof uses those structural results substantially; it does not claim a new neighborhood or a new output-count bound.
- Kenig, [Enumerating Small Potential Maximal Cliques with Fixed-Parameter Delay](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24856), live primary record remains v1, submitted 17 October 2026. Actual supplied Theorem 1.1 gives 2^{O(k^3)} n^4 log n delay for size-at-most-k outputs; setting k=n is not polynomial. Our claim covers all sizes but has no corresponding FPT guarantee, constrained search guarantee or claimed polynomial space.
- Grigoriev–Kobayashi–Tamaki–van der Zanden, [IPEC 2025 article 21](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.IPEC.2025.21), is the primary restricted-class comparison. Actual local Proposition 29 p.21:14 chooses a minimal triangulation saturating a separator and a clique on its other full-component side. Our helper is an arbitrary-graph constructive extension of this familiar idea; no novelty is claimed for separator extension alone. Their polynomial-delay theorem is for triconnected planar graphs.
- Capelli–Strozecki, [Geometric Amortization of Enumeration Algorithms, STACS 2023 article 18](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2023.18), abstract and primary record explicitly explain the folklore conversion from incremental-linear delay to polynomial delay when exponential storage is allowed. Their geometric method additionally controls space overhead. The current follow-up [From amortized to worst case delay in enumeration algorithms](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00037-026-00287-w), Computational Complexity 35, article 6, published 20 July 2026, was also opened at its primary publisher. Thus FIFO buffering, or the equality IncP_1=DelayP with permitted exponential storage, is emphatically not the new contribution here. We prove the needed incremental-linear property for this PMC generator, with termination, rather than infer it from total output-linear time.
- Berry–Blair–Heggernes–Peyton, [Maximum Cardinality Search for Computing Minimal Triangulations of Graphs](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-004-1084-3), Algorithmica 39 (2004), 287–298: actual local p.291 pseudocode, p.294 Theorem 4.4 and p.297 complexity verified. This is the polynomial helper primitive, not an algorithmic innovation of this project.
- Berry–Bordat–Cogis, [Generating All the Minimal Separators of a Graph](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/3-540-46784-X_17), WG 1999, LNCS 1665, 167–172: primary publisher record accessed; full older chapter not downloaded. Its polynomial-delay theorem is explicitly restated in actual local BLS Lemma 1. The graph-search queue recipe was also inspected in official igraph source, with the trie replacement and dequeue emission analyzed in research/polynomial_primitives.md.

Actual bounded searches included quoted combinations of potential maximal cliques, polynomial delay, output-linear time enumeration, incremental, and linear incremental; and direct searches for separator primitives and Capelli–Strozecki follow-ups. Primary arXiv histories and primary publisher records were opened. No general all-size polynomial-delay equivalent was identified in these results. Nonprimary aggregators were not relied on for mathematical or novelty conclusions. Search absence is not proof of priority; sources and versions are exposed for fresh review.

Proposed substantive delta: a full streaming algorithm for the original problem, using polynomial-fiber separator helper outputs to pay for a potentially exponential separator seed phase, injective recursive lifts at exact emission boundaries, and a simultaneous emission/termination induction with an additive recurrence. All individual structural/triangulation/buffering tools are known. The significance case is the all-graph worst-case-delay guarantee expressly absent from the nearest paper. Whether this combination is too routine or too narrow for publication/STOC is a genuine fresh-review question; no competitiveness verdict is asserted.
