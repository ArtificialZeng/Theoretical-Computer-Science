# Bibliography basis and citation map

No user-designated Excel workbook or BibTeX authority was supplied, as explicitly recorded in `literature/user_bibliography_check.md` and requirements.md. No workbook records were invented. Six genuinely relevant primary works are cited. Preprint records are pinned to the versions inspected; publication-status claims are unnecessary. Bibliographic access limitations do not suppress the mathematically accepted PDF.

- BCSZ18: Christian Borgs, Jennifer Chayes, Adam Smith, Ilias Zadik, *Private Algorithms Can Always Be Extended*, arXiv:1810.12518v2 (2018). [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12518v2); [Proposition 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.12518v2). Used only for the factor-two extension baseline on arbitrary metric input spaces. Metadata and the proposition were reopened during WRITE. The HTML's dynamically rendered date is not used as its bibliographic year; the pinned arXiv record establishes 2018.
- DRS19: Jinshuo Dong, Aaron Roth, Weijie J. Su, *Gaussian Differential Privacy*, arXiv:1905.02383v3 (2019). [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.02383v3); [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.02383v3), Theorem 2.10 (Blackwell comparison), Theorem 2.14 and Proposition 2.15 (continuous-group Laplace limit). Metadata and the relevant statements were reopened during WRITE. These are credited background, not a new unrestricted-reachability contribution or a dependency of the three proofs.
- LR15: Bas Lemmens and Mark Roelands, *Unique geodesics for Thompson's metric*, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 65(1), 315–348 (2015), DOI 10.5802/aif.2932. [Journal metadata and BibTeX](https://numdam.org/articles/10.5802/aif.2932/); [primary PDF](https://aif.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/aif.2932.pdf). Definition 2.1 gives the Thompson metric; Theorems 4.3 and 5.1 concern cone uniqueness; Theorem 5.2 concerns Hilbert geometry on a section. Metadata and primary PDF reopened during WRITE; actual theorem passages also documented in the accepted referee dossier. The normalized swap-path uniqueness is proved directly in the manuscript.
- Qiu26: Chenxi Qiu, *Interpolation-Based Optimization for Enforcing ℓp-Norm Metric Differential Privacy in Continuous and Fine-Grained Domains*, arXiv:2601.09946v1 (2026). [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09946v1); primary HTML inspected from the preserved research input `literature/privacy-interpolation.html`. Definitions 5–6, Proposition 3 and Appendix B.5 establish the normalized construction and privacy accounting used in the comparison. The abstract record was refreshed during WRITE. This citation does not assert that normalized coordinates are monotone.
- LQ26: Ruiyao Liu and Chenxi Qiu, *A Graph-Based Framework for Extending Metric Differential Privacy Mechanisms*, arXiv:2609.14125v1 (2026). [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14125v1); [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14125v1), Section 5.2 and Appendix E. Cached primary HTML `literature/privacy-framework.html` and its extracted passages explicitly include the pre-normalization ε/2 calibration, normalization, and endpoint envelopes. Metadata refreshed during WRITE. The original preprint's sample ACM DOI/ISBN/conference fields are not bibliographic metadata and are omitted; only the verified arXiv record is cited.
- FHMZ26: Alfonso Fernández de Bobadilla, Mykhailo Hontarenko, Guillem Müller-Rigat, Karol Życzkowski, *Reachable sets under Kolmogorov dynamics in the probability simplex*, arXiv:2609.38382v1 (2026). [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38382v1); [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38382v1), Proposition 6 and Theorem 8. Both were reopened during WRITE. Used for the one-sided instantaneous cost and its optimized quasi-distance; symmetrizing instantaneous costs before path optimization is a different operation. No all-budget peak formula is attributed to that paper.
