# Fresh primary-literature comparison

Reviewer job: c029-vftfast-referee-95ddbe77df2f. Checked 2026-11-02 UTC.
This is a bounded literature check, not an exhaustive priority certificate.

1. [Parter, Popova and Tzalik, New Fault-Tolerant (alpha,beta)-Spanners, arXiv:2610.33934v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33934v1), submitted 24 October 2026. The live arXiv history lists only v1. Theorem 1.4 and printed pp.4--5 give the target mixed-stretch size guarantee and identify the path-enumeration runtime challenge. The actual proof dependencies were read in the frozen text: Claim 4.3, Algorithm 2, Lemma 4.12 and its induction, Proposition 4.14 and Appendix A, Observation 5.8, Lemmas 5.9 and 5.14, and Theorem 5.15. The source's Corollary 3.20 uses a substantially larger fault exponent. Its packing machinery is prior work; no new sparsity theorem is attributed to the candidate.

2. [Dinitz and Robelle, Efficient and Simple Algorithms for Fault Tolerant Spanners, arXiv:2002.10889v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10889v2), 24 May 2020, PODC 2020. Theorems 2, 4, 5, 8, 9 were compared with the frozen local text. The size is O(t f^(1-1/t) n^(1+1/t)); the worst-case time is O(m t f^(2-1/t) n^(1+1/t)). These are polynomial uniformly after parameter caps. The candidate's protected-path gap test is a direct adaptation of their length-bounded-cut approximation, not a new approximation primitive.

3. [Bodwin, Dinitz and Robelle, Optimal Vertex Fault-Tolerant Spanners in Polynomial Time, arXiv:2007.08401v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.08401v3), 31 October 2020, SODA 2021. The optimal-size purely multiplicative guarantee was checked in the live abstract and frozen local text (Theorem 1.3 and its context). It provides no O(sqrt(k)),O(k) guarantee at the n^(1+1/k) density exponent for all f. The candidate uses the simpler DR construction; its extra factor in t is allowable logarithmic slack.

4. [Fomin, Lokshtanov, Panolan and Saurabh, Efficient Computation of Representative Sets with Applications in Parameterized and Exact Algorithms, arXiv:1304.4626v4](https://arxiv.org/html/1304.4626v4), 22 February 2016; published JACM 63(4), article 29, DOI 10.1145/2886094. Sections 2.5 and 3, especially Theorem 3 and its minor-vector proof, already give the representative-family algebra. Sections 5.1 and 5.5 give path-DP applications. The candidate's elementary specialization is correct but does not establish novelty of representative families or of path extension followed by reduction.

5. [Karthik C. S. and Parter, Deterministic Replacement Path Covering, arXiv:2008.05421v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2008.05421), 9 April 2023 (SODA 2021 extended abstract; later TALG version). Theorems 2 and 28 and Section 7 were inspected as an additional close comparison. Hit-and-miss families already provide fault-avoiding short-path coverings, including a vertex variant. In particular the f-dependent power proportional to path length is an established phenomenon. The multiplicative and nearly additive spanner applications there have different tradeoffs; no uniform construction of the present PPT mixed-stretch/fault-size guarantee was identified. These results can replace parts of a path-representation implementation; that observation reduces the case for new primitive-level depth, but does not supply the complete regime-switch argument or supersede its resulting theorem.

6. [Bilo, Chechik, Choudhary, Cohen and Schirneck, Simpler and Improved Replacement Path Coverings, arXiv:2604.27966v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.27966) and [Bilo, Choudhary, Cohen and Schirneck, Near-Optimal Replacement Path Coverings, arXiv:2608.07124v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.07124), primary introductions and stated guarantees inspected. They improve covering values/query mechanisms, principally for edge-fault RPCs. Neither record states the candidate's vertex-fault mixed-spanner result or its parameter switch. They reinforce that short-path/fault separation itself should not be advertised as a new general tool. No reduction from their stated guarantees alone to the entire target was found in their text.

Searches included the source title/identifier with polynomial and running-time terms; fault-tolerant spanners with representative sets/families/paths; and replacement path covering with short paths, faults and polynomial time. Search hits from secondary sites were used only as leads; the substantive comparisons above rely on primary papers. No equivalent complete construction was identified in this bounded search. Very recent or unindexed work could be missed. This general limitation does not establish a known duplicate or an unresolved mathematical hypothesis.
