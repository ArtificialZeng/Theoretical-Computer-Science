# Truly Subquadratic 3SUM over Every Bounded-Exponent Abelian Group

**working_paper** · 8 total pages.

[PDF](c034-group3sum_working_paper.pdf) · [Unified index](../../README.md)

Gives a randomized truly subquadratic algorithm for three-list zero-sum detection and witness recovery over every compact finite abelian group of fixed bounded exponent and polynomial order. The exponent saving depends on the exponent bound, independently of the polynomial order parameter. Simultaneous original-coset profiles and an extremal divisor certificate balance all torsion orders around a prior thin-matrix engine. The algorithm has a worst-case time cap and one-sided success probability at least 3/4, and implies subquadratic algorithms for every fixed abelian-group-representable finite coordinate relation.
