## Referee assessment

The new content is not the known witness or its unary polynomial behavior.
It is the simultaneous reduction of arbitrary singular copyless maps to
affine coordinate permutations on a subquotient that retains the minimal
weighted action. The polynomial spectral projection is what makes
observable-quotient invertibility usable without assuming physical
invertibility. The commuting-unipotents consequence then excludes the
exact witness after a finite-control return-word reduction.

This is a substantive structural advance and a complete answer to the
specific expressiveness question. It is not a routine application of
the inspected weighted-ambiguity or semiring-specific separation results.
The assessment supports local mathematical acceptance of the frozen
resolution; it makes no prediction of venue acceptance.
