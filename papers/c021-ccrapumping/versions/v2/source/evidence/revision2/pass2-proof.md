# Revised research proof: unipotent weighted functions and finite control

Proposer: c021-ccrapumping-research-b9b720512ca2, research pass 2,
2026-11-01 UTC. This is an unreviewed proof dossier. It requests a new
frozen-packet review; no previous acceptance is used for this extension.
The immutable source question is still resolved by the proposed rational

## Statements and conventions

All fields and constants in this extension are Q. Write a weighted
representation in row convention as f(w)=lambda M_w gamma, with
M_uv=M_u M_v. Minimal means both spans lambda A and A gamma are full,
where A is the unital Q-algebra generated by the letter matrices.
The zero function, whose minimal dimension is zero, is handled separately.

**Theorem 1.** Suppose the minimal representation of a nonzero recognizable
function f:Sigma* -> Q has dimension d and EVERY word matrix M_w is
unipotent: (M_w-I)^d=0. The following are equivalent:

1. f has a deterministic finite-state polynomial Q-CCRA, with any finite
   number of registers and arbitrary rational constants, allowing singular
   and nonlinear copyless updates;
2. its minimal letter matrices commute pairwise;
3. f is invariant under every permutation of input positions;
4. f is a rational polynomial in the letter counts.

**Theorem 2.** The all-word-unipotence condition on the MINIMAL
representation can be decided in polynomial bit time from a rational
weighted representation, after standard exact minimization. Inside this
effectively recognizable sector, Q-CCRA membership is decidable in
polynomial bit time. No decision claim is made outside that sector, and
no polynomial-size synthesis claim is made for general compact weighted
representations.

**Theorem 3.** For input f(w)=sum_p c_p [w:p], with explicitly listed
finite words p over an explicitly given finite alphabet and binary-encoded
rational coefficients, Q-CCRA membership is decidable in polynomial bit
time. Here [w:p] counts scattered embeddings, and [w:epsilon]=1. Membership
is equivalent to permutation invariance. Put D=sum_p(|p|+1). On YES,
one can construct a one-state copyless machine of polynomial encoding size
with at most sum_p |p| registers. On NO, one can return words uabv and
ubav, each of length at most 2D, with equal Parikh counts and different
f-values. An empty term list represents zero and is a separate immediate
YES case. Zero coefficients, repeated patterns, cancellations and empty
patterns are permitted.

## 1. Dependency on the one-state obstruction, checked from its proof

Sections 1--5 of `../resolution-proof.md` prove the following lemma:

> If the minimal weighted letter matrices of a one-state polynomial
> Q-CCRA are unipotent, then they commute.

This pass rechecked that argument, rather than taking an old review as a
premise. Its essential steps and adverse checks are as follows.

* Pullback preserves the vector space of squarefree monomials because
  coordinates of every copyless tuple have disjoint variable supports.
  Composition remains syntactically copyless, before cancellation.
* Every nonconstant multiaffine polynomial over Q is onto Q. Disjoint
  supports make a copyless tuple onto its coordinate affine image L;
  the squarefree pullback has rank 2^(number of nonconstant outputs).
  This allows resets, products and negative coefficients.
* At a minimum-rank word e, P_e restricted to L has exactly as many
  nonconstant outputs as input variables. Each output therefore depends
  affinely on exactly one distinct free variable. Only that restriction
  is invertible; physical P_e need not be.
* Normalizing by its affine coordinate inverse gives a copyless
  retraction Q onto L. Its pullback E is the projection onto im B_e
  along ker B_e, and is polynomial in B_e by the Chinese remainder
  theorem. On any invariant observable subquotient where M_e is a unit,
  (I-E)B_e=0 implies E acts as identity. Thus compression does not
  assume physical invertibility or discard the minimal representation.
* E B_a E is pullback by Q o P_a restricted to L. The word eae forces
  this map to have the same minimum number of active coordinates, hence
  it too is an invertible affine coordinate permutation. The same
  invariant subquotient realizes the original minimal matrices.
* After a common factorial power, the coordinate permutations disappear.
  The unipotent factors of these coordinatewise affine maps are
  translations in a common coordinate system and commute. Multiplicative
  Jordan factors are polynomials in the pullback matrix, so they preserve
  both invariant spaces and descend to its subquotient. Unipotence of
  the descended matrix kills its semisimple factor. Taking finite
  polynomial unipotent roots recovers commutation of the original letters.

The pullback order is h -> h o P; the identity for compression is exactly
C_a J(h)=h o Q o P_a o Q=J B_(Q o P_a|L)(h). No algebraic-group image
theorem or rectangular-length bridge is a dependency. The zero-free-
coordinate case has a one-dimensional squarefree space and is harmless.

## 2. Finite-control transfer retaining the ENTIRE minimal algebra

Assume a finite-state Q-CCRA computes f under Theorem 1's hypothesis.
Choose a reachable bottom SCC C in its complete deterministic control
automaton. Reading words gives transformations of this nonempty closed
set. Choose a word t whose transformation has smallest image size r>=1.
Its square also has rank r, so t permutes its image S. Set N=r! and
e=t^N. Its image R=S has size r and e fixes every element of R.
This also applies when t and e are empty words: the identity is among
the transformations considered.

For each original letter a, consider the word eae, read left to right.
Its image on C is contained in R. Its image on R equals its image on C:
the first e maps C onto R and is identity on R. Minimality of r forces
this image to equal R. Thus eae restricts to a permutation of R, and
h_a=(eae)^N fixes every state in R. The additional word h_*=e also fixes
all these states. Choose one q in R and an ACTUAL prefix p reaching q.
There is no need for a bound on |e| or N: these words prove existence,
and the membership algorithm below does not construct this control.

The register maps for h_* and h_a, the valuation after p, and the output
at q give a one-state copyless machine for g(z)=f(p h(z)) over a new
block alphabet. Composition of updates is copyless. Its displayed
weighted representation on the original d-dimensional space has initial
row lambda M_p, final column gamma, and matrices

    B_*=M_e,        B_a=(M_e M_a M_e)^N.

Let B be the unital Q-algebra generated by these block matrices. Each is
an original word matrix and hence is unipotent. Certainly B is contained
in A. Conversely, M_e^{-1}=sum_(j=0)^(d-1) (I-M_e)^j belongs to B.
For U_a=M_e M_a M_e, the all-word hypothesis ALSO makes U_a unipotent.
The finite binomial identity gives

    U_a=sum_(j=0)^(d-1) binom(1/N,j) (B_a-I)^j.

Indeed substitute X=U_a-I into ((1+X)^N)^(1/N)=1+X modulo X^d.
Therefore U_a is in B and

    M_a=M_e^{-1} U_a M_e^{-1} is in B.

Consequently A=B. This equality, rather than an assumed faithful action
of the control monoid, is the decisive transfer. It uses unipotence of
ALL word matrices, including eae and p; testing only the original
generators is not sufficient to establish this hypothesis.

The block representation is still minimal in dimension d. Its observable
column span is B gamma=A gamma=Q^d. Since M_p is a unit of A, with inverse
the same finite nilpotent polynomial, M_p A=A. Its reachable row span is

    lambda M_p B=lambda M_p A=lambda A=(Q^d)^*.

The span of monoid words equals the unital algebra span, so these really
are the reachable and observable spans of g. By minimality, the one-state
obstruction from Section 1 applies to the displayed B matrices themselves
up to simultaneous similarity. They commute, making B a commutative
algebra. Since A=B, the original M_a commute. This proves (1)->(2) for
arbitrary finite control and arbitrary register count.

For d=1 all statements above still hold. For zero f, use a zero-register
constant-zero one-state machine. No rank, root, or minimality claim is
required in dimension zero.

## 3. Completing the equivalences

If the M_a commute, write M_a=I+N_a with N_a^d=0. For every word,

    f(w)=lambda product_a [sum_(j=0)^(d-1)
                           binom(|w|_a,j) N_a^j] gamma.

This is a rational polynomial in the counts. Conversely every such
polynomial is invariant under permutations. If f is invariant, then
lambda M_u(M_a M_b-M_b M_a)M_v gamma=0 for all u,v,a,b. Reachability
and observability of a minimal representation imply the commutator matrix
is zero. This also proves the equivalence with adjacent-swap invariance.

Finally, every finite rational polynomial in the counts has a one-state
copyless realization: for every occurrence of a count variable as an
operand in each expanded monomial, allocate a separate register initially
zero and increment it exactly on its corresponding letter. Use each
allocated register once in its monomial, multiply by its rational
coefficient, and sum these disjoint products in the output. Each update
uses each old register only in its own new register. Constants need no
registers. This proves existence, with no efficient-size assertion for an
arbitrary polynomial obtained from compact matrices. Theorem 1 follows.

## 4. Deciding the all-word promise and membership

For rational d-by-d matrices with d>=1,

    all M_w unipotent  iff  tr(M_w)=d for all words w.

The forward direction is immediate. For the reverse direction fix w.
For j=1,...,d the trace assumption for the word w^j gives tr(M_w^j)=d.
Newton identities determine the characteristic polynomial as (T-1)^d;
Cayley--Hamilton then yields (M_w-I)^d=0. No diagonalizability assumption
is used.

Form the augmented vectors (vec(M_w),1) in dimension d^2+1. Right
multiplication acts linearly as (vec(X),c)->(vec(X M_a),c). Starting from
(vec(I),1), build their invariant span by breadth-first basis closure.
The condition holds exactly when L(X,c)=tr(X)-dc vanishes on that span.
All retained basis vectors are actual word vectors with words of length
at most d^2: at any plateau the increasing word-length span is already
invariant. A failed test therefore returns a word of length <=d^2 whose
matrix is not unipotent. The converse trace criterion uses the universal
condition established by invariant closure, not a bounded search.

First minimize an arbitrary input WA by reachable and observable linear
algebra; otherwise redundant nonunipotent blocks could falsely reject
the promise for the function. The zero function is a separate YES case.
Minimal representations are unique up to simultaneous similarity, making
the tested property intrinsic. If the promise passes, test pairwise
commutators of the minimized letter matrices. Theorem 1 proves that this
decides membership in precisely this sector.

Here are explicit bit bounds. Let n be the supplied WA dimension and L
its total binary encoding length. Clear all input denominators by one
common integer of O(L) bits. Entries in any length-k raw matrix product
have O(k(L+log(n+1))+L) numerator/denominator bits: multiply the scaled
integer matrices and bound the at most n^(k-1) summands in each entry.
Reachable/observable basis words have length <=n-1; candidates generated
from them have length <=n. Exact rank tests and coordinate changes use
minors of such polynomial-bit matrices. Hadamard's bound and fraction-free
elimination keep their encoding length polynomial in n,L. This establishes
polynomial-bit minimization, not just an arithmetic-operation count.
The resulting minimal matrices also have polynomial-bit entries. The
trace-space retained words have length <=d^2 and generated candidates
length <=d^2+1; the same product and minor bounds apply. There are at most
d^2+1 basis vectors, with polynomially many letter extensions. Thus this
test, and the final commutator test, have polynomial bit complexity.
These are standard rational-series equivalence/minimization tools; their
use is not separately claimed as an innovation. Theorem 2 follows.

## 5. Explicit pattern input: exact decision and short certificates

For p=p_1...p_l use states 0,...,l and matrices

    M_a=I+sum_(i:p_i=a) E_(i-1,i),

with initial e_0 and final e_l. A transition can advance only one edge,
even when repeated adjacent pattern letters coincide. Ignoring letters
uses diagonal loops. This counts scattered embeddings exactly. Take the
direct sum of the pattern representations, multiplying each initial row
by c_p. Its dimension is D. All word matrices are upper unitriangular.
Unipotence descends to the reachable/observable subquotient, so Theorem 1
applies after minimization, including cancellations.

For the algorithm no minimized coordinates are necessary. Build a basis
of reachable rows r=lambda M_u and of observable columns c=M_v gamma,
retaining original word vectors. Both bases have words of length <=D-1.
Test r(M_a M_b-M_b M_a)c for every pair of basis vectors and every pair
of letters. If a scalar is nonzero, uabv and ubav witness unequal values;
each has length <=(D-1)+2+(D-1)=2D and equal letter counts. If all vanish,
bilinearity gives adjacent-swap invariance in every context, and adjacent
swaps generate all permutations. Theorem 1 then gives exactly the claimed
CCRA membership answer. A vanishing reachable or observable space is YES.

Let B be the total bit length of the rational coefficients. Matrix
entries in a word of length k count embeddings and are at most 2^k;
reachable entries multiply these by a single input coefficient. A common
denominator has O(B) bits. Retained vectors have words <=D-1 and generated
candidates <=D, hence entries have O(B+D) bits. D-by-D minors have
O(D(B+D+log(D+1))) bits. Fraction-free elimination and polynomially many
scalar tests establish polynomial bit time. This also accounts for
discarded dependent candidates, not only the retained basis.

## 6. Explicit pattern input: polynomial-size copyless synthesis

Order the alphabet and sort any w into consecutive letter blocks. On YES,
f(w)=f(sort(w)). A nondecreasing pattern p has exactly

    product_a binom(|w|_a,m_(p,a))

embeddings into this sorted word; any other pattern has zero. This holds
term by term, so signed coefficients and cancellations cause no problem.

For every retained (nondecreasing) p, letter a, and integer
0<=j<m_(p,a), allocate a DISTINCT register x_(p,a,j), initially -j.
Increment it by one when a is read and leave it unchanged on other
letters. Output the disjoint sum

    sum_(p nondecreasing) [c_p / product_a m_(p,a)!]
                          product_(a,j) x_(p,a,j).

This is the falling-factorial formula for the binomial coefficients,
valid also at counts smaller than m_(p,a). Every old register is consumed
by just its own update, and each register occurs in precisely one final
product. The formula is syntactically copyless; constants and empty
patterns are allowed. There are <=sum_p |p| registers, polynomially many
expression operands and update entries, and factorial denominators have
O(D log(D+1)) bits. This proves the polynomial-size and bit-time claims
of Theorem 3 without expanding a multivariate polynomial.

## 7. Binding to the full original question and limits

The original witness equals [w:1]+[w:01]+[w:11]: for each 1 at position i,
there are i-1 earlier letters and one contribution for the symbol itself.
It differs on 01 and 10 (values 2 and 1). Theorem 3 consequently supplies
another derivation of its nonrecognizability in the FULL finite-state,
arbitrary-register Q-CCRA model. Its weighted realizability and uniform
pumping formula, including a single R=Z[1/2] and m=1 for every u,w,v, are
proved directly in Sections 6--7 of `../resolution-proof.md`. Together
these disprove the immutable sufficiency implication, not a narrowed
bounded-register substitute. The pumping calculation is attributed to
