## Proposed structural statement

Let f:Sigma*->Q have a minimal weighted representation (lambda,M,gamma) in which **every word matrix M_w is unipotent**. Then f is recognizable by a finite-state copyless polynomial CRA iff the letter matrices M_a commute, equivalently iff f is a polynomial with rational coefficients in the Parikh counts of its letters. A promised-unipotent input would therefore admit an effective CCRA-membership test by finite pairwise commutation checks. This does not assert a method for testing the all-word unipotence promise, and does not replace it by unipotence of generators alone.

This proof depends on the one-state commuting-unipotent lemma in the currently proposed resolution-proof.md. That lemma is still under actual fresh review. The finite-control transfer below is a new obligation, not a previously accepted theorem.

## Proposed necessity: normalize the control monoid as well

Assume a finite-state CCRA realizes f. Choose a reachable bottom SCC C of its complete control DFA. Choose a word t inducing minimum image cardinality r on C among all word transformations, including the identity. Completeness gives r>=1. Every power of t still has image size r by minimality. Thus t restricts to a permutation on its image. Choose e=t^(r!) (the empty word is allowed if t is empty). Its transformation E on C has image R of size r and fixes R pointwise.

For each letter a, the transformation of eae maps C intoR. Its image equals its image onR, since the first e is ontoR and e|R=id. Minimality makes this image allR. Hence eae restricts to a permutation ofR. With N=r!, each block h_a=(eae)^N fixes every q inR. The block h_0=e also fixes every q inR. Fix such q and a prefix p reaching q. These return blocks give a one-state CCRA computing z->f(p h(z)), with block alphabet {0} unionSigma.

Let A be the unital Q-algebra generated by the original minimal letter matrices. The matrices of this block representation are

    B_0=M_e,
    B_a=(M_e M_a M_e)^N.

Every B_j is unipotent by the all-word assumption. Let B be the unital algebra they generate. Certainly B subset A. Conversely M_e^(-1) is a finite polynomial in B_0-I. The matrix U_a=M_e M_a M_e is also unipotent by the same all-word assumption. Its unique unipotent Nth root formula recovers U_a from B_a as a finite rational binomial polynomial. Thus

    M_a=M_e^(-1) U_a M_e^(-1) belongs toB,

so A=B. This is why unipotence only of the original generators is insufficient for the asserted argument.

The block realization is minimal on the original weighted vector space. Original observability means A gamma spans the full column space, unchanged since A=B. Original reachability means lambda A spans the full row space. Since M_p is unipotent, it is a unit of A with inverse inA; therefore M_p A=A, and lambda M_p B=lambda A also spans the full row space. So no dimension is lost when changing the initial row and restricting to the return blocks.

The one-state obstruction applies and makes all B_j commute. Then B is a commutative algebra. Since A=B, every original M_a commutes. This proves the proposed finite-control transfer, conditional on the separately reviewed one-state lemma.

## Proposed converse and polynomial formulation

Commuting unipotent letter matrices give

    f(w)=lambda product_a (I+N_a)^{|w|_a} gamma,

where every N_a is nilpotent and the factors commute. Expand each factor by its finite binomial sum (powers >=dimension vanish). This is a rational polynomial in the finitely many counts |w|_a. No optimal total-degree claim is needed.

Every rational polynomial in letter counts has a one-state copyless CRA: for each monomial allocate independent copies of each needed letter counter, increment each appropriate copy on its letter, and use their product with the monomial coefficient in the output. Sum the monomial outputs with disjoint register copies. Each old variable is used once in its own increment/update, and each copy appears once in the final output. Constants are allowed. This is a finite construction.

If f is commutative as a function, then f(uabv)=f(ubav) for every u,v,a,b. Minimal reachability and observability imply M_a M_b=M_b M_a. Conversely commuting letters make f commutative. Thus the equivalent formulations are consistent under the all-word unipotence promise.
