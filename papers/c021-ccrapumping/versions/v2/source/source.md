# Frozen original: sufficiency of the uniform CCRA pumping condition

Primary source: Filip Mazowiecki, Antoni Puch and Daniel Smertnig, Pumping-Like Results for Copyless Cost Register Automata and Polynomially Ambiguous Weighted Automata, STACS2026paper67, https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2026.67, Section3.2/Theorem15/Example26; fullversion arXiv2502.07356v2(12Jan2026).

Verbatim question context: “We conjecture that this is not sufficient to characterise functions recognised by CCRA, even if it is already known that the function is recognised by a weighted automaton.” The preceding uniform condition and the proposed Example26 witness are part of the frozen source.

Full target: determine whether the source's uniform necessary condition is sufficient for a field-valued weighted-automaton function to be recognizable by a copyless polynomial cost register automaton. More precisely, if f:Sigma*->K is weighted-automaton recognizable and there exist a finitely generated subsemiring R of K and one integer m>=1 such that for EVERY u,w,v the sequence (f(u w^{m(n+1)} v))_{n>=0} is an R-generable EPS in Definition11, must f be CCRA-recognizable? In characteristic0 include Theorem15's coefficient-sum condition for every degree (it follows from the generation property). A positive resolution covers the whole stated model; one valid field/function counterexample disproves the sufficiency assertion. A rational-field counterexample settles the source's insufficiency conjecture, not merely a bounded-register version.

R-generable EPS means closure under pointwise sum/product of constants fromR, n*1_K, geometric sequences alpha^n withalpha inR, and their geometric partial sums as inDefinition11. Copyless means each old variable occurs at most once across ALL coordinate expressions in each update and the output, allowing finitely many control states and arbitrarily many registers/constants. No restriction to linear updates, no fixed register bound, no sign restriction, no artificial one-state original. Full finite control and polynomial copyless updates are required.

SourceExample26: f(w)=sum of the positions (starting at1) of the1symbols in binaryw. The paper verifies the pumping side but explicitly leaves non-CCRA expressibility unproved. Example27's sum of2^positions IS CCRA-recognizable and is a known control, not a counterexample. The supplied minimum-rank compression argument is unverified prospective evidence and must survive adversarial proof/quotient/algebraic-group checks. Even if it fails, preserve full target; no manuscript for a subsidiary obstruction or finite search.
