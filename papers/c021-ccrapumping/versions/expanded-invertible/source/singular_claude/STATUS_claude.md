# STATUS (singular_claude) — 2026-11-02, ~07:30 Beijing (final)

## Bottom line
1. **Goal (B) is settled: CCRA over Q are not closed under reversal.** This proves MPS Conjecture 51 (arXiv 2502.07356v2), with complete proofs in `section_claude.tex` (Theorem `thm:affinecontext`, Corollary `cor:reversal`). Three referee passes by an independent subagent found no fatal error; all their fixes are applied.
   - **Witness over {a,#}:** g(u_0#u_1#...#u_k) = Σ_{i=0}^k Π_{j<i} |u_j|.
     - g is not CCRA.
     - g^r is computed by: one state; registers x, A, both initially 0; on a: A := A+1; on #: (x, A) := (x·A + 1, 0); output x·A + 1.
   - **The MPS Example 52 candidate is a witness:** F is CCRA, F^r is not.
   - **A second witness:** h(w) = Σ_i Π_{j<i}(|u_j|+1) is not CCRA, h^r is. Every word containing # has rank 2, and all maximal subgroups satisfy (3).
   - **Non-closure also shows up in random machines:** two random 2-state, 2-register CCRAs (seeds 12, 13) and a tiny one-state CCRA (printed in the section) have non-CCRA reversals.
2. **New necessary conditions for the singular case (goal C):**
   - **Prop `prop:invweight` (inverse weights).** For a return monoid acting invertibly on a local quotient: a unipotent translation from weight κ_2 to weight κ_1 forces a compressed register with multiplier κ_2/κ_1. That multiplier is a rational series of the word.
   - **Prop `prop:lifting` (rational lifting).** Translations of compressed registers are rational series too, and they determine the unipotent coordinates of ρ.
   - **Machine-free consequences:**
     - Affine contexts (Thms `thm:affinecontext`, `thm:affinegeneral`): a block scalar that is not a pure exponential along pumping rules out CCRA.
     - Unipotent contexts (Thm `thm:unipotentcontext`): a block translation that is not an exponential polynomial along pumping rules out CCRA.
   - **Ex `ex:symmetric`:** E(w) = Σ_i Π_{j≠i}(|u_j|+1) is not CCRA. Yet E is palindromic, its local groups are commutative and satisfy (3), and its inverse weights are trivial. So "(3) + rational inverse weights" is not sufficient.
3. **Goal (A): exact characterizations for two classes beyond the invertible sector.**
   - **Thm `thm:rankone`: every singular letter has rank ≤ 1.** f is CCRA iff the group generated by the invertible letters satisfies (3).
     - Decidable; closed under reversal.
     - So any reversal counterexample needs a letter of rank ≥ 2, and g shows rank 2 is enough.
   - **Thm `thm:onereset`: Σ={a,#}, M_a invertible, rank M_# = 2, every #-word of rank 2.** Write M_# = CR and T(n) = R M_a^n C ∈ GL_2. f is CCRA iff all of:
     - (a) the eigenvalues of M_a are roots of rationals;
     - (b) closure⟨T(n)⟩° is Q-triangularizable;
     - (c) if that identity component has unipotents, then T(n) = [[a,b],[0,d]] in the adapted basis with d/a and b/a linear recurrence sequences.

     The class contains h (fails d/a), E (fails b/a) and h^r (CCRA).
4. **Goal (C), more:**
   - Normal form (2) fails beyond the invertible sector: g^r is not computed by any affine CCRA, even with resets (Prop `prop:interact`).
   - Global compression gives nothing with resets (Discussion).
   - g and h satisfy (3) at every maximal subgroup of the closure monoid (Rem `rem:localcond`). So no transpose-invariant characterization exists, and the paper's "combinatorial condition on resets" has to be arithmetic.
5. **Not achieved:**
   - A characterization for all singular series.
   - An intrinsic (machine-independent) form of the lifting condition; an informal conjecture is in the Discussion.
   - Decidability of (b) in Thm onereset.
