# Truly Subcubic Exact Triangle over Bounded-Exponent Abelian Groups

**working_paper** · 8 total pages.

[PDF](c034-grouptri_working_paper.pdf) · [Unified index](../../README.md)

Gives a deterministic truly subcubic Exact Triangle algorithm over every compact finite abelian group of fixed bounded exponent and polynomial order. A capped witness listing constructs enough of a known frequency decomposition without cubic initialization; vertex gauges then cancel quotient carries and reduce the remaining constraints to a smaller-exponent kernel. Explicit construction costs and a recursive exponent ledger make the saving independent of the polynomial group-order parameter. This extends the elementary-group scope without improving integer APSP bounds.
