compilation records are not described as entirely unmodified official exports.

pmlr-v306-kim26l. The complete published PDF was checked at Sections5.1–5.2
and AppendixD.2, printed p30. The manuscript quotes only the permitted

source audit. LCL's pinpoint support-reduction reference explicitly uses the

identified through the displayed moment row. Khanafer is cited only for
horizon-moment information supported by the institutional abstract. No missing
source was called nonexistent. Besbes concerns known-radius shift regret;
DKTVZ already supplies per-instance robustness. The Cui–Dinitz distribution-
shift paper is distinct from their two-slope tail-risk chapter (publisher year
