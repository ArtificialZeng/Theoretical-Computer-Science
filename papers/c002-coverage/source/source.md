# Frozen target: size-independent non-additive Beck–Fiala bound

Primary source: Avila, Rohwedder and Wennmann, Non-Additive Discrepancy: Coverage Functions in a Beck-Fiala Setting, ESA2026; arXiv2602.09948v3, Definition4 and Section1.3 Future Directions, printed pp4–5. Full downloaded source is literature/source.pdf and source.txt. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09948v3

The paper proves a bound O(sqrt(t^3 k) log(ntk)) for k-color discrepancy and asks whether the logarithmic size factor can be avoided. Freeze the following original qualitative question before research:
Does there exist a fixed polynomial P(t,k), independent of n and the number m of functions, such that every t-sparse family of coverage functions on n items has a k-coloring with discrepancy at most P(t,k), for all positive integers n,m,t and k>=2?

Precise model: feature occurrences are indexed by (i,u), with nonempty incidence sets S_(i,u) subseteq[n]. Function f_i(T)=sum_u 1[S_(i,u) intersects T]. Each item j belongs to at most t FEATURE OCCURRENCES across all functions. Equal incidence subsets at different occurrences remain distinct for the degree count; this is the original covering-universes reading and the paper's claimed classical Beck-Fiala specialization. Empty features have zero contribution and may be removed. A coloring partitions [n] into color classes T_1,...,T_k. Discrepancy is max_(i,c,c') |f_i(T_c)-f_i(T_c')|.

Do not use a literal deduplicated union of equal subsets to produce an artificial t=1 counterexample: that would contradict the source's intended sparsity model and its own theorem. Explicitly flag any source ambiguity separately. Arbitrary real feature weights, merely bounded number of influenced functions, support-preserving allocation, or a fixed t/k restriction are NOT this target.

A positive resolution must prove one explicit polynomial for ALL n,m,t,k; constructive polynomial-time coloring is a welcome stronger payoff, but existence alone resolves this frozen question. A negative resolution must exclude EVERY fixed polynomial P (e.g. an unbounded-discrepancy family at fixed t,k), not just disprove an attractive O(t) bound. No finite search or bounded-rank special case resolves it. Do not claim removal of the source exact sqrt(t^3 k) overhead unless actually proved; an alternative polynomial t,k dependence must be stated honestly.

Admission: source-supported qualitative open direction, with bounded current exact/equivalent searches. Triage must recheck literature and whether a size-independent polynomial is already known. First decisive route: use degree counting/Hall's theorem to allocate disjoint rainbow blocks to all large feature occurrences, then inspect whether the remaining small features can be rounded in independent block classes with a multiple-choice Beck-Fiala argument.
