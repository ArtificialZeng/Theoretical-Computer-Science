Verbatim Question9.2: "Do there exist upcycles with d > 1?"

## Precise unchanged mathematical reading
Does there exist ANY finite alphabet A of size a>=2, integer n>=1, and nontrivial finite cyclic partial word u over A union{*}, such that every total word of A^n is compatible with exactly one cyclic window u_i...u_(i+n-1), and each window contains a constant number d>=2 of wildcard characters? Nontrivial means at least one ordinary symbol and one wildcard; indices are cyclic modulo the full word length. A wildcard matches any letter independently. Exactly once counts starting indices, not equivalence classes or multiplicities after identifying rotations.

A positive answer requires a single explicit valid witness (a,n,d,u), with independent verification of ALL a^n words and all cyclic windows, and genuine novelty/value. A negative answer requires ALL finite alphabets, lengths and nontrivial frames, not a bounded search. Do not replace this full existence question with one selected parameter triple, conjecture9.1(d=1), a frame bound, or a construction-method failure. Source trivial-all-wildcard exception is excluded explicitly by Section2.

## Nearest source results
