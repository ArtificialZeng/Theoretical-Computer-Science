# Precise original target

The immutable question is `source.md`: He–Mani–Nie–Tung–Wei, arXiv:2512.11222v1, Problem 3.23 (printed p.17). For **every integer ℓ≥1**, decide whether the directed cycle Hℓ whose cyclic edge word is (++--) repeated ℓ times is locally tournament Sidorenko (LTS). It has 4ℓ vertices and 4ℓ edges; plus means clockwise and minus means counterclockwise. At ℓ=1 the explicit four-cycle word fixes the length-two base convention. This is the directed 2-subdivision of the alternating 2ℓ-cycle.

For each ℓ the assertion is: there exists εℓ>0 such that for every integer n≥1 and every real matrix A∈[0,1]ⁿˣⁿ with A+Aᵀ=J, writing C=A−J/2, if ρ(C)≤εℓn then t_Hℓ(A)≥2^(−4ℓ). Diagonal entries equal 1/2. The homomorphism density is n^(−4ℓ) times the sum over all vertex maps f:V(Hℓ)→[n] of the product of A[f(v),f(w)] over directed edges v→w. Maps need not be injective. Since C is real skew-symmetric, ρ(C)=||C||op.

With u=1/√n, P=uuᵀ, B=C/n and T=A/n=P/2+B, the full-family density is tr[(T²(Tᵀ)²)^ℓ]. The intake proposes the stronger global inequality tr[(T²(Tᵀ)²)^ℓ]≥16^(−ℓ) for all such A without any smallness assumption, and a quantitative forcing bound. Those stronger claims are proposals, not accepted mathematics. No fixed ℓ, bounded n, regular-only case, finite computation or failed sufficient criterion resolves the target.

Nearest inspected prior work: Grzesik–Iľkovič–Kielak–Kráľ, arXiv:2212.09343v3, Theorem 12 (printed pp.13–14) gives a coefficient sufficient criterion for cycle forcing; Fox–Himwich–Mani–Zhou, arXiv:2210.16971v1, Theorem 1.5, and arXiv:2402.08418v1, Theorem 4.2 give an asymmetric Sidorenko transfer requiring a homomorphism to a directed edge.
