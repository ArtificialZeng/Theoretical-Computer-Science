Verbatim mathematical statement (math typesetting transcribed):

> Problem 3.23. Let ℓ ≥ 1, and let Ĉ₂ℓ be the oriented cycle of length 2ℓ with alternating direction. Is Ĉ₂ℓ⁽²⁾ LTS or not?

Relevant original definitions, not changes to the question:

* Immediately preceding Corollary 3.21, a k-subdivision replaces every directed edge by a directed path of k edges oriented all in the original direction.
* Definition 3.1, printed page 7: LTS means there is ε=ε(D)>0 such that every n-vertex weighted tournament A=J/2+B, with spectral radius of the real skew-symmetric B at most εn, has t_D(A)≥2^{-e(D)}.
* Thus the full target is EVERY integer ℓ≥1 and ALL n and all sufficiently small skew perturbations that are weighted tournaments. Its cyclic edge word is (++--) repeated ℓ times. The length-four case gives the intended interpretation of the length-two alternating base.

No one value of ℓ, bounded n, finite evidence, necessary condition, or theorem restricted to regular tournaments resolves this original family. A global weighted-tournament lower bound would resolve the entire question more strongly. Novelty and significance remain to be reviewed.
