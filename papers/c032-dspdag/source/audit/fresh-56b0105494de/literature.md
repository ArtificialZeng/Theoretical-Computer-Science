* [Akmal, Vassilevska Williams and Wein, arXiv:2404.15916v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15916v2): the current arXiv page still lists the 10 May 2024 revision. * [Eppstein, Finding Common Ancestors and Disjoint Paths in DAGs, TR 95-52, 15 December 1995](https://www.ics.uci.edu/~eppstein/pubs/Epp-TR-95-52.pdf): freshly opened author-hosted full text, Sections 2--4, especially Lemma 2 and Theorem 2. Section 3 compares the Perl--Shiloach ordering convention. Thus tuple semantics and terminal devices are established prior tools; the report does not supply this candidate's batched transition cost.
