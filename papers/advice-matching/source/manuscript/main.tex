\begin{abstract}
\normalsize
We study unweighted online bipartite matching when every realized vertex has degree at most two and advice is an injective partial matching table that may contain absent edges. Against an oblivious adversary, we determine the largest robustness attainable at every requested consistency $c$: it is $\min\{3/4,3/2-c\}$ on this class and $\min\{7/8,7/4-c\}$ on its 2-regular subclass. Consistency holds for every maximum matching supplied as advice. The central algorithm gives priority to available offline neighbors whose second incident edge has appeared, and uses advice only among fresh neighbors. It is optimal under maximum-matching advice and retains a deterministic $3/4$ guarantee on cycles under arbitrary advice. Combining this endpoint with the known $7/8$ cycle baseline gives the cycle frontier; common advice tables on small indistinguishable inputs give matching upper bounds.
\end{abstract}
