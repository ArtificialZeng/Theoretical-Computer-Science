# Strongly Polynomial Near-Linear Preprocessing for All-Target Restricted Shortest Paths

**working_paper** · 9 total pages.

[PDF](c042-strongrsp_working_paper.pdf) · [Unified index](../../README.md)

Studies directed restricted shortest paths with nonnegative lengths and costs, one source, and a fixed cost budget. A deterministic representation provides a bicriteria (1+epsilon) approximation for every feasible target after near-linear preprocessing with polynomial dependence on inverse accuracy. Its arithmetic-operation count is independent of weight magnitudes, aspect ratios and precision; witness decoding has a separate deterministic polynomial-time bound. The construction combines a stopped two-clock oracle, offline activation, conservative rounding and bounded-space decoding. The accuracy dependence is deliberately loose.
